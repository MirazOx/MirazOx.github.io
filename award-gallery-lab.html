Compare & pick the best animation strategy

With 6 consecutive Dean's List scholarships, here are 5 distinct presentation strategies shown inside the actual column card width (~360px). Interact with each one directly to see how it feels.

1 · 3D Coverflow Carousel

Linear Streak · Perspective 3D · Direct Scrub
Recommended
Features the active certificate front-and-center with 3D angled neighbors.
  • Interaction: Click/drag cards, or tap the semester pills at bottom to jump directly.
  • Why it works: Crystal clear chronology without visual clutter. Shows each certificate large and legible.

2 · Splayed Arc Poker Fan

Playing-Card Hand · Tactile Hover · Arc
Splays all 6 awards in a neat, symmetrical -25° to +25° playing-card arc anchored at the bottom.
  • Interaction: Hovering or dragging across any card glides it forward and lifts it up high.
  • Why it works: Feels physical and prestigious ("holding a royal flush"), without clipping out of the card.

All 6 certificates sit in an overlapping horizontal row. Each card exposes its vertical term spine.

4 · Kinetic 2×3 Unfold Grid

Clean Stack → Full Gallery Unroll
Starts as a neat single stack showing the latest award. On tap, it smoothly unrolls into a 2-column by 3-row grid.
  • Interaction: Tap "View all 6 terms" to unfold; tap again to fold back.
  • Why it works: High editorial polish. Gives visitors full readability of all certificates side-by-side.
