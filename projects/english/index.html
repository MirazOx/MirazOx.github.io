How these work

Each engine asks you to produce the answer, then brings back what you miss until it sticks.

Active Production

Each tool asks you to type or commit to an answer before revealing it, so recognition never passes for recall.

Sentence Variation

A missed item comes back in different sentences testing the same point, so you learn the pattern rather than one card.

3 · 10 · 30d Spaced Rechecks

Items you recover return for typed rechecks at 3, 10 and 30 days before they count as mastered.

Offline-First & Client-Side

Everything runs in your browser. Progress is saved on your device, and error logs export as a printable worksheet.
